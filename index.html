What Is the Vantage Client Portal?

The Vantage Client Portal is the online account area for eligible Vantage clients. It provides access to account-related services and information. Depending on the account, legal entity, and location, users may be able to review trading accounts, find platform login details, manage profile information, and access funding or support features.

Vantage operates through different legal entities, and its legal documents and regulatory arrangements vary depending on the entity associated with the client's account. Review the entity and documents shown during registration and within your account.

How to Log In to Vantage Markets

Vantage's help guidance describes Client Portal login using the registered email address or phone number and password. Follow these general steps:

Open the official website

Enter vantagemarkets.com in your browser and check the domain carefully.

Choose the login option

Open the Client Portal sign-in page from Vantage's official website.

Enter your registered details

Use the email address or phone number registered to your Vantage account, together with your password.

Complete security checks

If Vantage requests additional verification, follow the instructions shown on its official page or app.

Open your Client Portal

After authentication, use the account tools available for your profile and jurisdiction.

Sign out on shared devices

Log out after using a public or shared computer and do not save passwords on devices you do not control.

Security reminderThis website does not provide a Vantage login form and will never ask you to submit your email, password, trading account number, or verification code.

Vantage MT4 and MT5 Login

Vantage MT4 Login

Open MetaTrader 4, select File → Login to Trade Account, and enter the MT4 trading account number, trading password, and assigned server. Make sure the account is an MT4 account.

Vantage MT5 Login

Open MetaTrader 5, select File → Login to Trade Account, and enter the MT5 account number, trading password, and assigned server. MT5 credentials are not the same as Client Portal credentials.

Vantage says the trading account number and server are typically provided by email when a live or demo account is opened. They can also be found in the Accounts section of the Client Portal or Vantage app. If the assigned server is not listed, contact Vantage support through its official channels.

How to Keep Your Vantage Account Secure

Use a strong, unique password and keep your login information confidential. Avoid signing in through links in unexpected emails, text messages, or social media posts. Check the website address before entering credentials, keep your devices updated, and avoid using public computers for financial accounts.

Vantage's login guidance specifically reminds clients to keep their login details confidential. If you suspect unauthorized access, contact Vantage through the contact details published on its official website and follow its account-security instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in to my Vantage account?

Vantage says clients can sign in through the Client Portal on its website or the Vantage mobile app using their registered email address or phone number and password.

Are Vantage Client Portal and MT4/MT5 passwords the same?

No. MT4 and MT5 use a trading account number, trading password, and assigned server. These details are separate from the Client Portal sign-in credentials.

Where can I find my MT4 or MT5 account number and server?

Vantage says these details are typically sent by email when an account is opened and can also be found in the Accounts section of the Client Portal or Vantage app.

Can I use MT4 details to sign in to MT5?

Use the platform that matches the trading account. Check the account type and assigned server in the Client Portal before signing in.

Can US residents open a Vantage Markets account?

The international Vantage website states that its services are not available to US citizens or residents. Check the latest official regional restrictions for current information.
